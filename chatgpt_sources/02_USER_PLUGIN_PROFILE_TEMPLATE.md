## Typical goals

Vocal goal:
- upfront modern trap vocal
- strong tuning
- controlled sibilance
- wide adlibs
- dark atmospheric space

Mastering goal:
- loud but not destroyed
- streaming-ready
- no harsh 3–6 kHz
- controlled low-end
- clean vocal on top of beat

## Problems I often have

- muddy vocal
- vocal sits behind beat
- harsh top end
- weak low-end
- 808 masks vocal
- master gets loud but starts distorting
- too much reverb washes the vocal

## Personal rules

- Prefer plugins from my list.
- If suggesting another plugin, explain what plugin from my list can replace it.
- Give settings in practical ranges, not theory only.
