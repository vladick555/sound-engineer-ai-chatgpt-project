# Copyright and Safety Rules

## Manuals

Do not include full copyrighted manuals in the public repository unless you own the rights or have permission.

Safe:

- official links
- short notes written by you
- your own workflows
- your own examples

Unsafe:

- copied full PDF manuals
- copied paid course text
- leaked plugin manuals
- paid preset packs
- copyrighted sample packs

## Audio files

Do not upload commercial tracks, stems, acapellas or samples unless you have rights.

For examples, use:

- your own audio
- royalty-free demo files
- short synthetic examples
- text reports instead of actual audio files

## Screenshots

User can upload screenshots into their own ChatGPT Project for analysis. Do not include private user screenshots in the public repo unless permission is clear.

## Claims

Do not claim:

- professional mix guaranteed
- mastering fixed automatically
- exact settings work for every vocal
- AI replaces human engineering

Do claim:

- helps analyze settings
- suggests practical starting points
- explains mix problems
- adapts to user's plugin list

## Privacy

User profile files may contain private gear, habits, artist plans or unpublished track info. Tell users to remove sensitive information before publishing forks or screenshots.
