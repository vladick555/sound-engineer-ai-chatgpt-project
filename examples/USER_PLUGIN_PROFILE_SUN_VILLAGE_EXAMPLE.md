## Sound goals

Lead vocal:
- upfront
- tuned
- dark but clear
- not muddy
- not harsh

Adlibs:
- wide
- atmospheric
- more wet than lead

Master:
- loud
- clean
- controlled low-end
- no painful harshness

## Common problems

- vocal sounds muddy
- beat masks vocal
- master gets loud but loses quality
- reverb pushes vocal backward
- high-end becomes harsh
